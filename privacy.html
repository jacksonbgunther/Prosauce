Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Toon Sauce (“we,” “us”) runs toonsauce.com. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join our launch list, plus where you signed up (for example, the top or bottom of the page).
  • Your answer to our optional question about how you usually get your protein, if you choose to answer.
  • Browsing information collected through cookies and similar technologies, such as pages viewed, your IP address, browser and device type, and Meta cookie identifiers.

How we use it

We use your email address to send you launch updates and occasional news about Toon Sauce. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. We use browsing information to understand how the site is used and to measure and improve our advertising.

Meta Pixel and Conversions API

We use the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversions API to measure and improve our ads on Facebook and Instagram. This means we share information with Meta Platforms, Inc., including a hashed (SHA-256 encrypted) version of your email address, your IP address, browser user agent, Meta cookie identifiers, and browsing events such as page views and sign-ups. Meta may use this information as described in its own privacy policy. You can manage how Meta uses this information in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Who else sees your data

We use an email service provider to store our list and send emails. We don’t sell your email address.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? message us on Instagram at @toonsauce.
